# 2026 Week 1 forecast review

Week 1 of the **2026 NCAAF** season is complete (99/99 ESPN games, Aug 29–Sep 7). **NFL Week 1 has not started** (first game NE @ SEA, Wed Sep 9). This review grades every NCAAF game the model could price, the live profit-gated book, the tickets that were actually published, and the sat NFL card.

Prices for the reconstructed book are ESPN core **closes** (DraftKings). Published Friday/Saturday tickets used live DraftKings numbers at snapshot time and can differ from the close. All results are ESPN finals. Flat 1u.

Reproduce: `npm run grade:week1`

## Headline

The model did **not** beat Week 1. The profit-gated NCAAF book finished about break-even with a large calibration miss: it kept assigning ~61% to sides that hit 50% of the time.

That is a **small sample**. It is **not** evidence to retune the gate, unsit NFL, or chase last week's losers.

The 80% moneyline number is **not** a win. Week 1 is full of FBS-vs-FCS smash spots. Against the **spread and total market**, the model lost. That is the relevant test for a +EV desk.

## Calibration

Chronological booked sides: mean P **61.6%**, hit **50.0%**, gap **-11.6pp**, Brier **0.262**.

Week-level: mean P **61.4%**, hit **50.0%**, Brier **0.263**. Reliability bins are all 0.55–0.65 predicted, 0.50 actual.

This is the same disease as the 2025 trusted walk-forward (P high vs realized hit). The profit gate was built to *throw away* the worst of that (MLs, 20-point “edges”). It did not make P honest. Week 1 did not fix it and did not prove it is fatal. 26 decided bets at 50% vs 61.6% is well inside binomial noise (se ≈ 10pp).

## Recommendations

Principle-driven. Lowest-overfit next step first.

1. **Keep the live book as-is.** NCAAF SRS + profit gate, NFL sat, small / quarter-Kelly size. Week 1 is not a holdout replacement.
2. **Do not add a new filter from this weekend's losers** (no “ban 3-leg parlays”, “ban Week 1 totals”, “fade 30-point favorites”). Those would be curve-fits to 6–27 bets.
3. **Do not unsit NFL** and do not promote House EPA. Its 2025 research holdout is still negative.
4. **Hold out Weeks 2–4** before any gate change. Want ≥100 live NCAAF bets, or a full month, before touching alignment/edge/EV caps.
5. If anything is adjusted later, the *pre-declared* candidate remains shrinking P / widening sigma, not deleting markets that lost last Saturday.

## Counterfactual: if weekly metrics existed before Week 1

The original review priced Week 1 with **SRS + rest + a wind stub**. Injuries, archive weather, process leftovers (pass-rush, explosives, fumble luck, CFB red zone), and multi-book tape were not on the number.

This is not a retune. Same profit gate, same NFL sit, same 2024–25-only ratings. Reproduce: `npm run grade:week1-metrics`.

What we are allowed to use as “known before kickoff”:

- NCAAF: 2024–25 SRS, 2025 sportsdataverse process cards, **historical Open-Meteo at kickoff**. Current ESPN injuries/news/Action Network are **Week 2 state** and would leak, so they are not applied.
- NFL: Sep 6 House snapshot vs the same snapshot plus nflverse process leftovers plus the current Week 1 injury/weather ingest (games have not kicked; this is pre-game). Still sat.
